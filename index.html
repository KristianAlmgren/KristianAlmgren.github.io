Process

Firstly

Creating the concept

Define behavior, verbs and rules to get a base on what could be. Document and expand.

Secondly

Prototyping the concept

Locate rewards and states of failure in which the player could get stuck at.

Lastly

Executing the concept

Balancing and tuning the numbers of the game to fit the style and create notes for further research.
